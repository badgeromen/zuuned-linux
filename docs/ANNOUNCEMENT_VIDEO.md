# Zuuned Linux announcement video

## A 60 to 90 second launch video

Lead with the real device working. Show the app and Zune together where possible.

| Time | Picture | Suggested narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–10 seconds | Zune connected by USB; app discovers it | “This is my Zune. And this is it working on Linux.” |
| 10–25 seconds | Browse a small music library, choose an album | “I built Zuuned to get my collection back onto a player I still love.” |
| 25–45 seconds | Queue an album, show transfer complete, eject cleanly | “Browse your collection, build playlists, and send your music over USB.” |
| 45–60 seconds | Play the transferred track on the physical Zune | “It works for me. Now I want other people to try it.” |
| 60–75 seconds | Brief video, photo and customization views | “It also handles local videos, photos and artwork.” |
| 75–90 seconds | Show the GitHub download page and Health export | “The code is public. Grab a Linux testing build, tell me your Zune model, and send a debug report if something breaks. Your device. Your music. Your rules.” |

Use the app features actually present in the download being announced. The
migrated 0.1.1 AppImage predates some changes in the public source; record it if
that is the release linked in the video. If showing a newer development build,
label it and explain that the downloadable version differs.

## Before recording

- Use a small demonstration collection with media you can share publicly.
- Hide personal file paths, account names, device serials and private metadata.
- Capture app sound and narration separately; keep music below the voice.
- Record the physical Zune playing after a clean eject, not just a success toast.
- Let every USB transfer finish before stopping or restarting the application.
- Show the release's Linux compatibility requirements briefly and label it a
  testing build. Avoid claiming every distribution or Zune model has been tested.

## Video title and description

Title: **I brought my Zune back on Linux. Meet Zuuned.**

Description:

Zuuned is a native Linux app for managing Microsoft Zune players and enjoying
your local media collection. It works for my setup, and I am opening the source
and testing builds for other people to try.

Download testing builds: https://github.com/badgeromen/zuuned-linux/releases
Application source and issues: https://github.com/badgeromen/zuuned-linux
C library: https://github.com/badgeromen/libzune

Read the selected release notes for system requirements. For a bug report,
include your build version, Linux distribution, Zune model and reproduction
steps. In the app, export a report through Settings → Health and review it
before sharing. Reports are not uploaded automatically.

Independent community project; not affiliated with Microsoft.
